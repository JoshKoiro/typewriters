## Notes

I purchased this from the wife of a psychotherapist who was the original owner and purchased this in Europe probably not long after the time it was manufactured.
Since then, it had been used and _"well loved"_.

There was a receipt in the case that said it had it's platen replaced back in 1982 for $14! It is pretty hard now, and might be a typewriter that I get the platen professionally redone.

It came with the original case along with the original dust cover. Both are not in the best condition, but lovely that they are the original.

I have wanted a Lettera 22 ever since I first started getting interested into typewriters, and to be able to get one that has this beautiful Bodoni typeface is very exciting for me.

The experience of typing on a Lettera 22 is a bit different than the 32 (which has been my only experience of Olivetti up to this point), and I think I like it a bit more. Both Lettera's have that little "squish" when the keys are bottoming-out that feels very satisfying and soft on the fingers, though the Lettera 22 seems to be a little more smooth in my opinon. Of course, every machine is different as I am learning with every machine I lay fingers on, so one machine is not always a perfect picture of every specimen of that model.

Time will tell for sure, but I could easily see this machine becoming by daily typer.
