## Notes

This has needed some adjustment since I got it. I originally purchased this thinking it would be a bit of a "project typewriter", which it has been, but I was surprised just how quickly I was able to get typing on it after just a few tweaks and some good cleaning. I really love the look of this typewriter with the glass keys and the small profile. One of the most aesthetic to have on a shelf. The wrinkle paint is okay, though I think the car-style paint from this era looks better on these typewriters.
Came with one original metal spool (pictures show aftermarket plastic ones)
The case is a little ripped on the side, but still latches and functions as it should. It also came with the original cleaning brushes.
