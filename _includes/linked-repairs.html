{%- comment -%} Finds the repairs that point at one issue. Usage: {% include linked-repairs.html machine=m issue=issue %} Sets: linked_repairs (array, oldest first) A repair points at an issue with `issue: `, or `issue: [id-one, id-two]` if one job dealt with several issues. Repairs without `issue:` are ad-hoc work (cleaning, new spools...). {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign linked_repairs = "" | split: "" -%} {%- for r in include.machine.repairs -%} {%- assign rids = r.issue | join: "," | split: "," -%} {%- if rids contains include.issue.id %}{% assign linked_repairs = linked_repairs | push: r %}{% endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- assign linked_repairs = linked_repairs | sort: "date", "last" -%}
